# SM Platform: current state of the app

State at 17 October 2026, branch `main`. This file is the full picture that does not fit in the Lovable Knowledge. Read it before touching anything. Business rules live in the Knowledge; this file says what exists, where it lives and how it was built.

## How this repo is built

- Most of the app was built outside Lovable, directly in this repo, and is covered by 390+ Vitest tests (`npx vitest run`). The tests describe the expected behavior of the data layer: treat them as the specification.
- Lovable does presentation only (layout, responsive, visual consistency) unless a prompt explicitly asks for more.
- Never touch `src/lib`, `src/locales` or tests unless the prompt says so. If you see a logic or data problem, list it at the end of your answer and do not fix it.
- Reference screens for visual consistency (built in Lovable): Mis tareas, task detail, owner home, embudo board, lead detail.
- Demo date is fixed: `TODAY = 2026-10-14` (`src/config/app.ts`). "Ver como" switches the current user; it lives in memory and resets on reload.

## Industries

Strategic Mates works only with construction trades, the same keys as the public assessment: `construction`, `remodeling`, `installations_repairs`, `maintenance`, `cleaning`, `other_related` (with free text `activity_other`). The list is data (`INDUSTRIES` in `src/lib/leads/pipeline.ts` plus labels in `pipeline.industries`): adding a trade later is adding an entry. Catalog entries marked "Construcción / mantenimiento" go to construction, remodeling, installations_repairs and maintenance only.

## Entry: from the assessment to the program

1. The public readiness assessment lives outside this app (strategicmates.com) and arrives by webhook with the exact keys of `Lead` and `LeadResult` in `src/lib/api/types.ts`: business ranges (`revenue_range`, `margin_range` or null = "No lo sé", `team_range`, `years_range`), `hispanic_owner` (yes / no / talk, informational only), result with `readiness` ready | on_track, `band` high | mid | low | track (by the shown score: 70, 55, 0), shown `score` 40-92, `eligibility_gaps` (`years_operating`), seven blocks (`time_team`, `work_quality`, `money`, `clients_sales`, `information`, `experience`, `direction`) rated strong | medium | weak | unknown, strengths and priorities, `know_how_example`.
2. Zones: each state or city belongs to an office (SM Global edits them in Reglas; default office SM Miami). The lead gets the office's default consultant.
3. Phases shown in the lead, in this order: assessment completed, meeting booked (Google Meet), summary email sent, meeting done. The assessment sends no email: the person sees the result on screen with the "Agendar mi reunión" button (the consultant's calendar). Only the booking sends the summary email (headline, score, strengths, priorities) together with the date, time and Meet link of the meeting, so `summary_sent_at` never exists without a `meeting`. An evaluated lead without a booking shows "Esperando reserva", and "Sin reservar hace N días" from the second day after the assessment (`submitted_at`). The reminder to book after 2 days is sent by the future backend, not by this app. "Simular entrada" in the embudo creates leads and meetings for demos (SM only): "Simular assessment" creates the lead without email; "Simular reserva" books the first meeting, a new one after "No se presentó" / "Reagendar", or the expansion meeting of an accepted lead.
4. Stages of the embudo: evaluated, meeting, documents, accepted, in_program, discarded. Manual leads (referrals, events) enter as "Manual" without result; SM can send them the assessment.
5. Two NDAs, no friction (owner and Melany, 20 Oct: the client must sign, nothing to download). The booking creates the owner account (no password: the email carries the entry link `/entrada/$token`), the business in `onboarding` and Strategic Mates' NDA (kind `nda`), already signed by Strategic Mates with the office signature (rule `nda_auto_signed_by_sm`, signature PNG loaded in the office detail, "Firma de la oficina"). In "Tu ingreso" the owner reads a window that explains it (`Contract.explainer`: title, paragraphs, key points; "Strategic Mates ya firmó. Falta tu firma."), can read the full text (`Contract.body`, "Leer el NDA completo") and signs. Next to it, "Protege tu información": if he wants, he writes his own confidentiality agreement in the portal ("Escribir mi acuerdo": a plain textarea prefilled from `GET /businesses/{id}/own-nda/template`, with his business and his name filled in; SM Global edits the template in Reglas, `owner_nda_template`, with `{{business_name}}` and `{{owner_name}}`) and sends it ("Enviar para la firma de Strategic Mates", `POST /businesses/{id}/own-nda { body }`, contract kind `owner_nda`). He can change the text while nobody signed it (`PATCH …/own-nda`); codes `own_nda_exists`, `body_required`, `body_too_long` (20,000 characters). The consultant gets `owner_nda_received`, sees "NDA del dueño: por firmar / firmado" in the lead's checklist and signs it with the usual signer; then the owner sees "Firmado por Strategic Mates" with "Ver documento" and may "Firmar yo también". `IntakeSummary.own_nda` is `{ id, state, signed_by_sm, signed_by_owner }` or null. The owner's NDA never blocks the approval. The old replacement flow (upload a PDF, `propose-own`, `accept-own`, `reject-own`) is gone. A lead moved to Reunión by hand gets "Enviar NDA" and then "Enviar MOU" as its primary actions.
6. MOU at the NDA signature: the MOU exists as soon as the owner signs Strategic Mates' NDA (the owner gets `mou_ready`, which opens "Tu ingreso" on the MOU step, `/?step=mou`), so he can sign it during the meeting or before. The steps of "Tu ingreso" are NDA → MOU → Tu reunión → Documentos (`OWNER_STEPS`), then the expansion meeting and the Operating Agreement.
7. Meeting outcome: once the meeting started, its card in the lead has **Se hizo** (optional note), **No se presentó** and **Reagendar** (`setMeetingOutcome`, answered once: `outcome_already_set`). When it ended without an answer the portal asks (`outcome_prompted_at`): the embudo card shows "Resultado de la reunión pendiente" and the consultant gets `meeting_outcome_pending`, which opens the lead with the meeting card in focus (`/embudo?lead=…&focus=meeting`). "Se hizo" on the first meeting only sends the documents list and moves the lead to Documentación; "No se presentó" and "Reagendar" wait for a new booking.
8. MOU, guided: a window "Qué es el MOU" (its explainer), "Leer el MOU completo" (the full text in the document dialog, scrollable, by sections) and "Lo firmas en la reunión con tu consultor, o antes si ya lo leíste". Then the four points in plain words, each with its own "Entendí" (`funds_finance_intervention`, `business_stays_yours`, `franchisor_entity_participation`, `do_it_together_no_debt`); "Firmar" activates only with the four checked and saves them (`acknowledge`) before the signer opens. Signing a MOU without them answers `acknowledgment_required`.
9. Documents locked until the MOU: the due diligence documents go out with "Se hizo" and stay `locked_until_mou` until the owner signs the MOU (uploading earlier answers `mou_required`; "Tu ingreso" explains it). The list (Melany's 11 documents, 9 required), the mandatory flags and the review deadline are SM Global rules. Each document has its help (`intake_documents[].help: { what, counts[], tip }`): the owner and the consultant see what it is, a "Sirve:" list (for example the constitution: "Articles of Organization o Incorporation", "Certificado de Sunbiz", "Captura de Sunbiz con tu empresa activa") and a tip, under a general note at the top (`intake_help_text`: "No hace falta un documento armado: sirve una captura o una foto legible"). SM Global edits all of it in Reglas ("Ayuda para el dueño" per document, "Textos del ingreso"). The upload takes PDF, photos (PNG, JPG), Word and Excel, like the API. SM accepts each or asks for another with a comment. Review clock: 15 days by default, paused while a replacement is requested. Only admins (office or Global) approve the documentation. Until `in_program` the owner sees only "Tu ingreso" and Documentos.
10. Contract texts: every contract carries `explainer: { title, paragraphs[], bullets[] }` and `body` (full text for the NDA, the owner's NDA and the MOU). The document dialog ("Ver documento", `SignedContractDialog`) shows the body by sections, also before anyone signed.
11. Bishops (terna rule): from NDA + MOU signed the lead is an opportunity (view only, assessment data plus one verified fact per accepted document). SM Global decides in Reglas who sees it (`opportunity_visibility`): "presented" = only the Bishops SM presents it to with "Presentar a Bishops" (accepted leads, active Bishops with the framework signed; each gets `opportunity_presented` and sees "Strategic Mates te presentó este negocio"), "all" = every Bishop with the framework signed (the mock default). The investors section of the lead shows presented, viewed, passed and accepted. "Aceptar oportunidad" only in accepted; the first Bishop takes it. The owner's contact is never shown to a Bishop.
12. Expansion meeting: after the approval the owner books the "Reunión de ampliación" from "Tu ingreso" (longer, the Bishop joins); it is answered with the same three buttons. With `require_expansion_meeting`, "Enviar Operating Agreement" (the tripartite contract, kind `tripartite`) stays disabled with the reason until it was held (`expansion_meeting_required`).
13. Operating Agreement waiting period: nobody signs it before `oa_waiting_days` (7 by default) from the day it went out (`waiting_period`, with the date in `detail`); the owner sees "Podrás firmar desde el …" and SM sees the date on the contract row. Then funds received in full, and `in_program` automatically: day 1 and tasks generated from the catalog.

## Program

- Diagnóstico (days 1-15), six areas in parallel (B1 to B6), Cierre y handoff (days 150-180). Every program gets the 48 "minimo" tasks that apply.
- "Ya lo tenía": the program is still 180 days at the same price, with fewer tasks. Tasks of the areas the assessment rated strong start as "A validar: el negocio ya lo tendría" (block to area map editable by SM Global in Reglas). In the Diagnóstico SM decides per task: "Ya lo tenía" (evidence required; approved with the tag "Ya existía", no firm involved, counts for franchisability) or "Hay que hacerla". SM can also mark "Ya lo tenía" on any task not started, flagged by the assessment or not.
- Franchisability = approved tasks over total, with a goal (85 %) and a list of mandatory tasks set by SM Global (may be empty).
- Area health: red if a task is overdue; amber if something is due within 7 days or a deliverable waits for the owner over 7 days; green otherwise; grey if the area does not apply.

## Calendar

Calendar v2: the portal schedules meetings itself, in Google Calendar, for every role.

- Who connects: everyone (owner and team, firm, SM, Bishop, franchisee) from the Google Calendar card of Mi perfil. The access is read and write: the portal reads free time to offer common slots and creates in the organizer's Google Calendar the meetings scheduled here, with the Meet link. When Google stops accepting the access the card shows a bordered amber note with "Volver a conectar" (`calendar_reconnect_required`). `User.calendar_connected` says who is connected (older rows: `google_calendar_email`).
- The dialog (`ScheduleMeetingDialog`, the same everywhere): participants pre-filled from where it opens, plus anyone visible from a small people picker; topic; 30, 45 or 60 minutes. Then the slot picker asks `availability` for the next 14 days and shows only the slots where everyone is free, grouped by day in the viewer's time, with one line per person whose calendar could not be read ("Mariana todavía no conectó su calendario: le llegará la propuesta para confirmar"). Confirming creates the meeting; the note says "Te llegó la invitación de Google con el link de Meet" or "Queda propuesta hasta que X la confirme". `slot_unavailable` shows "Ese horario se acaba de ocupar, elige otro" and reads the slots again. Other codes: `participant_not_visible`, `calendar_write_scope_required`, `meeting_in_past`, `too_many_participants` (8 people, organizer included).
- Fallback (`planMeeting`): organizer connected, the event goes to Google with the Meet link; confirmed when every guest is connected, proposed for the ones who are not. Organizer not connected: a proposal every guest confirms, nothing in Google. The dialog still opens: "Proponer reunión" instead of "Agendar reunión".
- Where it opens: task detail (the task's firm person and the owner), the home agenda of a firm ("Agendar reunión", picking the task), the ficha del negocio and Mi negocio ("Agendar con mi consultor"), the lead detail (the lead's consultant and the owner, only when the owner already has an account; without one "Agendar reunión" keeps opening the booking link), and the Bishop's portfolio (the consultant of each funded business, `BishopInvestment.consultant`).
- Calendar screen (Mis tareas, calendar view, `?meeting=` focuses one): due dates, Google events, entry meetings and the portal meetings of every kind (`GET /me/meetings`), with status chips Propuesta / Confirmada / Cancelada and a "Google" mark. A meeting opens a side panel: "Entrar a Meet", "Mover" (the slot picker again) and "Cancelar" (confirmed inside the panel, reason optional) for the organizer or SM; anyone else gets "Avisar que no puedo" (a comment on the task, or a mail to the organizer). It opens without tasks too, so a Bishop gets there from the bell.
- Notifications: `meeting_scheduled`, `meeting_moved`, `meeting_cancelled` open the calendar with the meeting focused; `calendar_connect_suggested` and `calendar_reconnect_required` open Mi perfil.
- Rules: SM Global sets `meeting_hours` (start, end, time zone from a short list of US zones) in Reglas. Default 09:00-18:00 America/New_York.
- Mock: Carla, Lucía, Nicolás and Jefferson connected, Mariana not, Camila must reconnect; fake busy time per person; four sample meetings (Nicolás with Carla, Lucía with Carla on B6-03, Mariana's proposal to Carla, Jefferson with Nicolás). The old task proposals (`proposeMeeting`, `confirmMeeting`) keep working and still show in the task detail.

## Signing (DocuSeal, simulated)

- The signer draws, types or uploads a signature; "Firmar" needs a signature and shows the consent text. Decline needs a reason.
- Any contract with at least one signature opens as a signed document: the text, one signature block per party (image, name, date, or "Pendiente de firma"), the audit trail (sent, opened, signed, declined with dates) and the document fingerprint. "Descargar PDF" prints only the document.
- Everything goes through the repository (`openContract`, `signContract`, `declineContract`), so the real DocuSeal embed replaces the component without touching the rest.

## Money

The small business pays nothing. The Bishop pays SM the program amount (SM Global rule, today USD 150,000) outside the app; the app records it with date, method, reference and receipt. SM distributes it per business to the firms (one-off, installments or milestones; pending or paid with receipt) and keeps a share. Money is visible only to SM (office and Global) and that Bishop. No payment gateway. Amounts are shown without cents.

## Organizations, roles and accounts

- Tree: SM Global, SM offices by zone (Miami, Orlando, Tampa), businesses, their franchises. Partner firms belong to an office.
- Roles: `sm_global`, `sm_team`, `partner_firm`, `business_owner`, `bishop`, `franchisee`, plus `User.is_admin`.
- SM Global creates anyone anywhere; the office admin creates consultants of the office and partner firms (one active firm per area; "Reemplazar firma" chooses what happens with open tasks); the firm admin invites its people; the owner invites their team and can hand the business over. Nobody is deleted: people are deactivated with a reason and can be reactivated; everything stays in a history.
- Access: the `Session` repository has `login`, `logout`, `logoutAll`, `requestMagicLink`, `exchangeMagicLink`, `exchangeEntryLink`, `exchangeInvitation`, `acceptInvitation`, `forgotPassword`, `resetPassword`, `isAuthenticated`, and two-step verification: `verifyTwoFactor`, `recoverTwoFactor`, `setupTwoFactor`, `confirmTwoFactor`, `disableTwoFactor`. Login, magic link, entry link and accepted invitation answer a `SignInResult`: the User (session started), `{ two_factor_required, challenge_id }` (→ `/verificacion`, 6-digit code or "Usar un código de recuperación") or `{ two_factor_setup_required, setup_token }` (→ `/activar-verificacion` with `X-Setup-Token`: secret and `otpauth_url`, confirm with a code, the 8 recovery codes shown once with "Copiar códigos"; then sign in again). The challenge and the setup token travel in the router history state, never in storage. Mi perfil has "Verificación en dos pasos: activar / desactivar". The entry link (`/entrada/$token`, `POST /auth/entry-link/exchange`) is the link of the booking email: it signs the owner in and lands on "Tu ingreso". Demo: Marilyn has two-step verification on, code `123456`, recovery codes `SMDEMO-0001` to `SMDEMO-0008`; `magic-setup` (Nicolás) asks to activate it; entry links `entrada-castillo`, `entrada-rivera`, `entrada-expired`. The mock starts logged in (demo, tutorials, Lovable preview) and "Ver como" still switches the user; after `logout` the guard sends to `/login?next=`. Every seeded account logs in with `Demo1234!`; demo tokens live in `src/lib/api/mock/session.ts` (`invite-yesenia`, `invite-expired`, `invite-used`, `magic-carla`, `reset-carla`). Error codes: `invalid_credentials`, `account_inactive`, `token_invalid`, `token_expired`, `token_used`, `weak_password` (under 12 characters), `terms_required`, `name_required`. A failed login always shows a bordered message with what to do.
- Scope: SM team sees everything in its office; the owner only their business; a firm only the tasks assigned to it (or where it collaborates), never the business numbers, manual or documents.
- Route guard: each role reaches only the screens of its menu, plus Mi perfil and the task detail. Typing another role's URL goes back home.
- Verified business data (legal name, address, city, state): the owner edits them only during onboarding. Afterwards they are read-only with the mark "Verificado"; the owner asks for a change with a reason and SM applies or rejects it from the ficha, with a note for the owner. The data layer rejects direct edits (`locked_field`).
